AI-Assisted Business Analysis  ·  Project 02

What drives customer value in this e-commerce business — and where should it focus next?

Using AI to accelerate analysis while keeping validation and business judgment human-controlled. Shopee Thailand transaction data, 2022–2025 (synthetic). 300,000 orders across 55,822 customers with at least one completed purchase, analyzed with SQL as the primary analytical layer and Python for validation and visualization.

Customer Value

Which customers create the most value, and is a standard RFM score enough to find them, or does the data need a different approach? Purchase frequency turned out to be the answer — tested against category, product, and seller cuts, and it held up every time.

A pure additive RFM score was tested and rejected: it mixed newly-registered and one-time high-spend customers into the same tier as genuinely repeat customers.

03

Seller Performance

Are outcomes concentrated among particular seller types, and is the revenue difference between FBS and Standard logistics real? Toggle below — the raw numbers and the normalized numbers tell two different stories.

Raw revenue / seller Normalized: revenue / product
FBS60 sellers · 16 products each
Standard140 sellers · 28 products each
Raw view: Standard sellers appear to generate ~72% more revenue than FBS sellers (฿17.5M vs ฿10.1M avg). This is the number a first pass would report.
Finding — FBS and Standard sellers have a deterministic catalog-size difference (16 vs 28 products, no exceptions) baked into this dataset. Once revenue is normalized per product, the gap nearly disappears (฿634K vs ฿624K) — the raw comparison was measuring catalog size, not logistics performance. Separately, Company sellers do show a real, moderate revenue-efficiency edge over Individual sellers (~18–20% higher per product/customer) that catalog size does not explain — though Individual sellers attract customers with higher overall purchase frequency, so this is an association worth investigating, not a verdict.
→

So what should the business prioritize?

Prioritize frequency growth Focus retention and engagement initiatives on regular/occasional customers rather than category-specific targeting — frequency is the lever the data actually supports.
Targeted re-engagement, not a broad campaign A small group of 547 historically high-frequency customers (0.98% of the base, ฿48.2M in past value) have gone 180+ days without a purchase — small enough for direct outreach.
Investigate Company sellers' efficiency edge The ~18–20% gap over Individual sellers isn't explained by catalog size — worth a qualitative look at pricing, presentation, and fulfillment practices before acting on it.
Don't act on the raw FBS/Standard gap It's a catalog-size artifact. Any seller-support decision should be based on normalized metrics, not raw seller revenue.
Limitations — This analysis is observational; findings describe associations, not causal effects. Customer segments (B0–B6) are analytical business rules built for this project, not an existing CRM system. The dataset is synthetic. Full methodology, SQL, and a reproducible Python notebook are in the project repository.
